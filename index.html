Questions, answered

Got questions? We’ve got answers 24/7

How does your service work?

We offer a free estimate and service in Sonoma, Napa, North and West Marin Counties. Our expert Rat Detective will find the entry points of rats and mice into your home or structure. We’ll catch what’s there and then seal them out. We guarantee our work for 2 years. RES is 100% poison-free and does not use any rodenticides or pesticides.

What happens when there is activity after the 4 weeks?

RES service will be extended until there is no more activity. Our rat detectives will be executing perimeter checks during each service check. If there is any breach in RES work, or a new entry point is found, it can be addressed.

Do you provide exclusion service for animals other than rats or mice?

No. RES service for rats and mice only. For any other animals we recommend contacting Sonoma Wildlife Rescue, Wildcare or a local wild animal service provider in your area.
